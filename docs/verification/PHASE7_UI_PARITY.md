## Scope and release consequence

Daily Galla and UPI are expressly authorized core additions and were implemented through the existing facades. Missing calendar, staff, documents, advanced settings, home-delivery and bundle/modifier workflows are documented product gaps; they were not invented or represented as finished during a production-only hardening sprint. Their absence prevents UI-parity acceptance and the master product-completeness claim. No full offline POS, bank verification, automatic QR payment confirmation or WhatsApp integration is claimed.

Current browser checks establish functional Retail flows and specified viewport behavior, not equal competitor polish. Remaining accessibility work includes legacy icon-only labels, some raw selects, duplicate local/global error banners, keyboard/contrast review across all legacy pages, and consistent loading/empty/retry states. A visual reviewer must approve each row after its gaps are addressed in a separately scoped product-completion review.
